Where to book: use the airline's own site when it is within about $50 of the agencies. If a flight is cancelled or rerouted, you deal with the airline directly; adding bags or moving up a fare level to get 2×23kg is easier; and the US 24-hour free-cancellation rule applies (book 7+ days before departure). Expedia or Booking.com can be a little cheaper, but changes and refunds then go through the agency, which is slower when flights are disrupted. "Compare all" opens Google Flights, where clicking a flight lists every site selling it with its price. For Plan B, book both tickets on the same airline's site.
