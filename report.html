Booking tips

  • Book on the airline's own site unless Expedia or Booking.com is more than about $50 cheaper. If a flight is cancelled or rerouted, you then deal with the airline directly.
  • To get 2 bags of 23 kg, compare the airline's next fare level with paying for bags. The upgrade is often cheaper.
  • If you book directly with the airline at least 7 days before the flight, US rules let you cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund.
  • For a round trip + one-way: book the round trip first, then book the Boston traveller's one-way on the same flight as the round trip's return.

Prices come from Google Flights, in US dollars, economy class, and change by the minute. Bag fees are estimates. Left out of every list: layovers over 3 hours, IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, Turkish Airlines. Confirm the final price before you pay.
